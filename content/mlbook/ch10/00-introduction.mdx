Artificial neural networks are computational models inspired by the structure of biological neurons. They form the backbone of modern deep learning, powering applications from image recognition to natural language processing.

This chapter covers the perceptron, gradient descent, backpropagation, and advanced architectures including Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM), Gated Recurrent Units (GRU), Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN).

> Without reading Chapter 3 (Probability and Statistics) and Chapter 8 (Regressions), you will not fully understand the content of this chapter.
