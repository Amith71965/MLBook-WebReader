Regression analysis is one of the most fundamental tools in machine learning and statistics. It allows us to model the relationship between a dependent variable and one or more independent variables, enabling prediction of continuous outcomes.

This chapter covers univariate and multivariate linear regression, polynomial regression, ridge and lasso regularization, and methods for evaluating regression models.
